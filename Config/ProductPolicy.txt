# What a built product may link and carry, per build profile and platform.
# script/build_manifest.py checks every product it builds against these rules, and fails a
# profile whose product breaks one. A new framework or entitlement is therefore a reviewed line
# here, never an accident. See docs/BUILD_AND_DISTRIBUTION.md.
#
# Platforms: macos, ios, watchos, tvos, visionos (simulator and device builds share a name).
# A profile or platform of * matches any. Text after # is a comment; keep the reason.
#
#   link         <profile> <platform> <Framework>   executables may link /System/Library/Frameworks/<Framework>
#   entitlement  <profile> <platform> <key>         executables may declare this entitlement
#   unsupported  <platform> <Framework>             no executable on the platform may link it, whatever `link` says
#   purpose      <Framework> <Info.plist key>       a Store-lane product that links the framework must declare the key
