## Scope discipline

Complete one observable journey and its qualification before opening another implementation front. A new public release advertises only supported, evidenced adapters; the remaining catalog may stay visibly specified. A paid/account/managed-capability experiment never becomes an accidental prerequisite for earlier milestones.
