# ADR-014 — Every platform host exists before its experiments

Status: accepted 2026-09-30 (CORE-013).

## Decision

The Mac, iPhone, Watch, and Apple TV hosts all exist from M1, ahead of the experiments that use them. The Watch and Apple TV hosts are smoke hosts: the catalog, an experiment's detail, and Readiness. Both use the Companions profile and install directly to their paired devices. The Apple TV host shares the iPhone host's bundle identifier, so a signing team needs no additional App ID. `script/test.sh` builds all four hosts and runs host smoke tests on macOS and in the iOS, watchOS, and tvOS simulators on every change, and CI builds all four. Experiments still add their own platform surfaces through their own tickets.

## Rationale and consequences

Work across the lab should move on all four platforms together rather than waiting for each experiment to introduce its platform. A host that already exists catches platform breaks, such as an SDK marking a framework unavailable on tvOS or watchOS, in the change that causes them, and it lets device installs be qualified before an experiment depends on them. This supersedes the earlier plan in which Watch and TV entered only with their experiments.

`script/test.sh` now creates and deletes simulators and runs a remote-driven tvOS UI test, so it takes longer. A device install still needs the device paired, in Developer Mode, and awake. No experiment on the Watch or Apple TV is implied by its host existing.

## Validation

CORE-013's smoke tests: `LabPhoneTests`, `LabWatchTests`, `LabTVTests`, and the remote-driven `LabTVUITests`, run by `script/test.sh`, plus the unsigned `LabTV` build in CI.
